Title Autonomous SSL Certificate Renewal Agent (ASCRA) — SSL / TLS Certificate Operations Automation
Description*
As-is State The current SSL/TLS certificate renewal process is entirely manual, spanning six disconnected steps across Dynatrace, Jira, Microsoft Teams, PKI email, manual X.509 verification, and ServiceNow. Each renewal takes 1–5 business days per certificate. The process is serial — only 1–2 renewals per day are achievable at peak. Engineers must log into multiple tools and interfaces to compile and submit requests by hand. Expiry waves cause production TLS outages when the manual queue overflows. The process produces incomplete audit trails, introduces human error at every step, and creates private-key exposure risk through manual handling. This highly time-consuming process limits the team's capacity to focus on higher-value, business-critical activities.
⚠ Current Manual Process — avg. 1–5 business days per certificate
Engineer: 1. Check Dynatrace → 2. Generate CSR manually → 3. Open Jira ticket → 4. Get PD approval → 5. Email PKI team → 6. Verify CER manually → 7. Raise ServiceNow CHG
To-be State By deploying ASCRA, an Agentic AI orchestrator (Azure AI Foundry · GPT-4o · MAF 1.0) automates 5 of the 6 renewal steps end-to-end. The single preserved human gate — Product Director approval via an Adaptive Card in Microsoft Teams — is the only required human action. ASCRA automatically: ingests Dynatrace expiry alerts, generates the CSR in an FIPS 140-2 L3 Hardware Security Module (private key never leaves Key Vault), opens the Jira ticket, emails the PKI team, performs a deterministic X.509 verification (CN, SAN, chain, expiry ≥ 365 days), and raises the ServiceNow Pre-Approved Change ticket. The cycle time drops from 1–5 business days to minutes per certificate. A fleet-scale batch coordinator handles 100+ concurrent renewals from a single expiry wave. Every tool call produces a tamper-evident, hash-chained audit record stored for 7 years in immutable Blob storage, ensuring full HIPAA / ISO 27001 / SOC 2 compliance. Eight non-negotiable guardrails (G1–G8) are enforced in code — not by the LLM — with zero waivers.

Proof-of-Concept (POC) Scope* The scope of the proposed POC covers the implementation of the full 6-step renewal workflow in the staging environment for 5 standard in-scope certificates, each from a different application managed by the MSO team. The selected certificates will include a mix of single-hostname and multi-SAN types, ensuring coverage across varied PKI submission patterns. Existing Dynatrace SSL-expiry monitoring and the established PKI email workflow provide the consistent baseline for validation and comparison. The POC validates: autonomous steps T1–T2 and T4–T6, the HITL PD approval gate via Adaptive Card, deterministic CER verification, idempotent retry behaviour, and end-to-end audit trail completeness — from Dynatrace alert to ServiceNow change ticket.
